Geocoding is dropping some Hartline consignees in Massachusetts and Connecticut onto the wrong side of the state, and rating then prices the wrong lane. Their stop-list spreadsheets carry ZIP+4 as a plain number, so 02134-1234 arrives as 21341234. We store that as "21341-0234" when it should be "02134-1234". Five-digit ZIPs that lost a zero are fine. The import runs everything through `normalize_postal_code` in `postal_codes.py`.
