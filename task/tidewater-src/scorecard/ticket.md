Carrier management says the September scorecards undersell carriers who had a lot of shipper-caused delays, and Blue Ridge Haulers is now threatening to drop two Hartline lanes over it. Blue Ridge had 7 on-time deliveries, 2 late ones coded CUST/CONS and 1 late one on them, and the scorecard shows 70.0% when it should show 87.5%. The number comes from `on_time_percentage` in `scorecard.py`.
